About Us

Welcome to Zhaib Tools, a website offering simple and free online utilities to make everyday writing tasks easier.

Our goal is to provide easy-to-use tools that work directly in your web browser, without requiring an account.

Our Tools

Our Word Counter helps you count words, characters and sentences. Our Case Converter helps you change text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

Our Mission

We aim to make useful writing tools accessible to students, writers, professionals and anyone working with text.

We are continuing to improve our tools and add helpful features.
